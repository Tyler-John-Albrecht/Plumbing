The Cost of Waiting

Most plumbing problems
double in cost overnight.

A slow drain. A dripping faucet. A water heater humming wrong. They're not minor. They're warnings — and every day you ignore them, the bill grows.

A Dripping Faucet

Wastes 3,000+ gallons per year and usually signals failing internal components that will blow completely without warning — often at the worst possible moment.

A Slow Drain

That slow gurgle is often a blocked sewer line in progress. Left alone, raw sewage backs up into your home. The cleanup alone runs $5,000–$15,000.

Average sewer backup remediation: $8,500
